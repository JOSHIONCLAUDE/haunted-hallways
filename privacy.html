Haunted Hallways — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Haunted Hallways is a free, single-player horror escape game. We collect nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no trackers. The game works completely offline.

What stays on your device

Your settings (look speed, volume, brightness, graphics quality, difficulty and the jumpscare switch) are saved only on your own device. We never receive them. Clearing the app's data deletes them.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information.
  • We do not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • The game does not connect to the internet while you play.

Scary content

This game contains horror themes, loud sounds and jumpscares. There is a setting to turn the jumpscare pictures off. It is a fan-made game and is not made by or connected to any other game or company. All characters, names and art in it are original.

Children

The game collects no personal information at all. Because it is a scary game, we suggest it for older children and up, and that a parent decides what is right for their child.
