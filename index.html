HAUNTED
HALLWAYSFIVE NIGHTS TO ESCAPE

You wake up locked in a room in a creepy old house. Grandma Hettie and Grandpa Orville live here, and they do not want you to leave. Find the keys and tools, sneak past them and escape before your nights run out.
Difficulty
This game is scary: loud noises and jumpscares. A fan-made game, not made by or connected to any other game.

How to play

The goal

Escape the house before you run out of nights. When Grandma or Grandpa catches you, a night passes and you wake up back in your room. Your items stay with you.

Hunters

Grandma Hettie is fast and hears everything: footsteps when you run, doors, dropped things. Grandpa Orville is slow and a bit deaf. He only reacts to loud noises, but he is tough and hard to stop.

Sneak

Walk or crouch to stay quiet. Running, slamming doors, dropping items and hammering are LOUD. The noise meter in the corner shows how loud you are. Close doors behind you.

Hide

Step inside a wardrobe or locker with USE and press USE again to come out. If they did not see you hide, they will walk away. If they saw you, they will check there!

Escape

Open drawers and cupboards to find keys, tools and parts. Use a key on its door, find the code for the study, pry boards off with a crowbar. Then pick a way out:
🚪 Front door: its key + a crowbar
⛵ Boat (back garden dock): boat key, propeller, fuel
🚗 Car (garage): car key, battery, fuel, a fuse for the basement fuse box (power for the garage button) and bolt cutters for the gate chain

Fight back

Find the crossbow and some bolts. A hit stuns a hunter for a while (Orville recovers faster). Throw a bottle or teddy to make noise somewhere else.
